### Question

May Allah reward you well, brother Abu Omar from the UAE says: What is the ruling on reading so-called horoscopes written in newspapers for the sake of entertainment, without believing in them?

### Answer

There is no entertainment in that. If one reads them for the purpose of rejection and refutation, there is no harm. There is no entertainment in that; how can one be entertained by falsehood? Either one refutes them, or rejects them, or crosses them out and draws attention to their falsehood.
