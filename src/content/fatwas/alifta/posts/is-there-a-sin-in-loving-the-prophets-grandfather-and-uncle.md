### Question: 
 
I followed a (TV) series about the grandfather of the Master of Mankind—upon him be the best of prayers and peace—and because of that, I grew to love Abdul-Muttalib and Abu Talib, just as Allah knows my love for the Messenger of Allah, may Allah bless him and grant him peace. So, do I incur any sin in this, given that they died upon other than Islam? And is there any sin upon me for loving them, or will I incur blame as a result of this love? Please advise me, and may Allah take charge of you with His care. May Allah bless our master Muhammad, and upon you be the peace of Allah.

### Answer:

The care shown by Abdul-Muttalib and the protection and support given by Abu Talib to the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, do not necessitate loving them. This is because Abdul-Muttalib died upon the religion of pre-Islamic ignorance (Jahiliyyah), which is associating partners with Allah (shirk), and Abu Talib knew the truth but did not act upon it. He refrained from pronouncing the testimony of faith (Shahadah) at the time of his death when the Prophet, may Allah bless him and grant him peace, asked him to say it, and instead said: 'He is upon the religion of Abdul-Muttalib.' Therefore, the obligation is to hate them for the sake of Allah and not to love them.

And with Allah is success, and may Allah bless our Prophet Muhammad, his family, and his companions, and grant them peace.
